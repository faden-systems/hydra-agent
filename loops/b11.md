## Problem and outcome

Three findings on the deployed supervisor (fda35164, issue #57):

1. Every turn's message carries the whole `factory/state.json` (`Supervisor.state_block`, 155 KB on 2026-10-08 =
   47k-58k tokens), so the Claude session's measured context grows by ~70k tokens on an idle timer turn
   (`logs/turns.jsonl` context_tokens 586,101 -> 655,968 -> 725,880 -> 795,784 -> 865,695 -> 935,607 for turns
   1055-1060) and, with three API calls per idle turn, the ledger's input total rises by ~210k per turn.
2. The compaction policy cannot act on an ordinary overage: `compaction_due()` is due only for the forced `COMPACT`
   flag or an `emergency_pending` (b8 requirement 6 made automatic rollover emergency-only), the token threshold only
   writes the `pending` status line, `over_ratio` is parsed and never used, and with `compaction.rollover_enabled`
   unset `run_compaction()` records `held: automatic rollover is disabled`, clears `pending` and removes `COMPACT`
   without an engine call (the manual `hydra compact` of 2026-10-08 09:58:22Z was held that way and printed a
   success-looking `compaction at ... 94586 -> None`). Quiet hours play no part: `quiet_hours_now()` gates only the
   persistent-mode daily restart.
3. Status showed two numbers that are one measure at two moments: `pending (tokens 935607 over 300000)` was turn
   1060's first-message context; `94586 -> unverified` was turn 1061's first-message context after the Claude
   CLI's own compaction (a `compact_boundary` record in the session transcript at 09:40:58Z), which status never
   reports; the CLI compacts near its window every ~13 idle turns and a held supervisor compaction appears to take
   the credit.

Outcome: an ordinary overage compacts before the next turn, always (due when the last measured Claude context is at
or above `over_ratio x threshold_tokens`, 1.25 x 300,000 = 375,000 by default, so the founder's 3x case is covered
by a wide margin); rollover is on by default, and when it is explicitly disabled the held outcome is loud and never
clears the schedule; status shows one number, the last measured context, with `due`/`forced`/`held`/`awaiting
verification` states and the CLI's own compactions; and the per-turn message carries a bounded digest of
`factory/state.json` (every track's one-line fields, the full entries of the tracks the batch touches, the newest
founder decisions) instead of the whole file, for every engine.

This supersedes, for the ordinary token/byte thresholds in both Claude per-turn and persistent modes, the sentence "Ordinary token
thresholds must not rotate sessions" of b8 requirement 6; the emergency rollover and its qualification (an actual
resume failure specifically for a too-long conversation on an account shown working) stay exactly as b8 defines
them. b7 requirement 4 (context_tokens drives scheduling, never aggregate input_tokens) and requirement 13 (the
memory-writing `[compaction]` turn followed by an atomic session-id replacement; no `/compact`) stay in force. b4's
quiet hours and 25% override, already dead for compaction since b8, are restated here as: quiet hours gate only the
daily restart; `over_ratio` is the due multiplier.

## Requirements

1. An ordinary overage is due before the next turn. `Supervisor.compaction_due()` returns `(True, "forced")` when
   `COMPACT` exists; else `(True, "emergency")` under b8's unchanged emergency rule; else `(True, "tokens")` when
   `last_claude_context_tokens()` is not None and is at or above `over_ratio x threshold_tokens` of
   `compaction_config()`; else `(True, "bytes")` when the `pending` record's reason is `bytes`; else `(False, None)`.
   Three guards apply to `tokens` and `bytes` (never to `forced`): the back-off after a FAILED rollover attempt
   (`failed_at` + `retry_after_s`), exactly as it suppresses `emergency` today; a pending verification (`verify` is
   set after a rollover until the next measured Claude turn settles it, so a stale pre-rollover measurement never
   re-triggers); and the engine family: the selected engine (`current_engine`, `family_of`) must be Claude, in
   per-turn or persistent mode alike (a persistent child is retired by the rollover as b8's procedure does), so a
   stale Claude measurement never compacts while Codex is selected. Nothing else gates it: not quiet hours, not the
   time of day, not the presence of queued events. `run_once` keeps its order (the compaction runs inside the
   writer lock before the turn for any queued events), so once a measured Claude turn lands at or above the due
   limit, the very next tick compacts and only then runs the next turn. A context between `threshold_tokens` and
   the due limit stays `pending` (informational) as today. `over_ratio` is read from `compaction_config()` (default
   1.25, configurable, must be >= 1.0; smaller values are clamped to 1.0 with one `log` line).
2. Rollover on by default; a held outcome is loud and keeps the schedule. `COMPACTION_DEFAULTS` gains
   `"rollover_enabled": True` and `Supervisor.rollover_enabled()` reads `compaction.rollover_enabled` with that
   default (an explicit `false` disables). When disabled, `run_compaction()` still makes no engine call and records
   the held outcome in `logs/compaction.json` `last` (`ok` null, `error` starting `held: automatic rollover is
   disabled`), but it keeps `pending` as it was, consumes `COMPACT` (so a forced request is not retried on every
   tick), and posts exactly one buildlog line per held episode through `buildlog(...)`:
   `hydra-manager: compaction held (rollover disabled): context <N> over <limit>; enable compaction.rollover_enabled`
   (an episode is the span from a held outcome until a measured Claude turn below `threshold_tokens` clears
   `pending`; the once-per-episode guard is a `held_posted` flag in `logs/compaction.json` that a clearing turn
   resets). `hydra compact` (manager/hydra `cmd_compact`): with no live supervisor loop (`supervisor_alive` false) and
   rollover disabled it prints `compaction held: automatic rollover is disabled (compaction.rollover_enabled is
   false)` and exits 1 without writing `COMPACT`; with no live loop and rollover enabled it schedules as today (exit
   0). With a live loop it waits for the outcome and prints exactly one of: `compacted at <at> (<before> input tokens
   before); the next turn verifies the reduction and `hydra status` shows it` (exit 0, a rollover that produced a
   new session id, `verify` set); `compaction failed at <at>: <error>; the session is unchanged` (exit 1, `ok` false);
   `compaction held: <error>` (exit 1, a held outcome); `compaction unverified at <at> (<before> -> ?)` (exit 1, any
   other outcome without a new session id). It never prints a success-looking line for a held or unverified outcome.
3. The CLI's own compactions are detected and recorded. After every Claude turn (per-turn and persistent; the
   memory-writing `[compaction]` turn included, whose scan reads the OLD session's transcript before the session id
   is replaced) and after every supervisor compaction (the new session's transcript), the supervisor scans the current
   session's transcript
   (`find_claude_transcript(self.path(".claude"), self.home, self.session_id())`) from the byte offset recorded at
   the previous scan (`logs/compaction.json` `transcript_scan: {"path", "offset"}`, reset to the file's start when
   the path differs from the recorded one or the file is shorter than the offset) and reads only the new bytes: it
   seeks to the recorded offset, reads to the end, calls `cli_compactions_since(<those bytes>, 0)` and records the
   recorded offset plus the consumed length (the acceptance spies on the call and requires exactly the appended
   bytes with offset 0);
   after the scan `transcript_scan.offset` equals the offset of the last complete line read (the file's size when it
   ends with a newline), so a rescan from a fresh supervisor finds nothing new. A boundary's `at` decides its age
   against the supervisor's outcomes (requirement 4): an older boundary discovered later never replaces a newer
   supervisor outcome as the status `last`. Every
   complete JSON line whose `type` is `system` and whose `subtype` is `compact_boundary` is one CLI compaction:
   appended to `logs/compaction.json` `cli_compactions` (a list, newest last, each `{"at": <the record's timestamp or
   the scan time when absent>, "turn": <the turn number the scan belongs to>}`, kept to the newest 50), and the turn
   record in `logs/turns.jsonl` gets `cli_compacted: true`. A missing transcript, an unreadable line or a scan error
   never fails the turn (one `log` line). The scan also runs right after a supervisor rollover, so the recorded
   path follows the new session id; when the offset is reset (a different path, or a file shorter than the offset) a
   boundary whose `at` is already recorded is not recorded twice; the list keeps the newest 50 records. An
   unreadable transcript (a read error, a directory at the path) is logged once and the turn completes.
   `cli_compactions_since(data, offset)` is the module-level pure function doing the parse (bytes in, the list of
   boundary records found after `offset` and the offset of the last complete line out; a partial trailing line is
   left for the next scan), tested directly.
4. One number in status. `compaction_status_line(home)` returns exactly one line of the form
   `context: <N> tokens (turn <n>) | due at <limit> | last compaction: <last>` plus at most one suffix, where `<N>`
   and `<n>` are the last measured Claude context and its turn (`context: unknown` with no turn part when no Claude
   turn measured one), `<limit>` is `int(over_ratio x threshold_tokens)`, and `<last>` is the newest of the
   supervisor's `last` record and the CLI's `cli_compactions`: for the supervisor's `<at> (<before> -> <after>)`
   with `<after>` as today (`failed` / `unverified` when unknown, and `held` when the record is held), for the CLI's
   `<at> (cli)`, or `none`. The suffix, in this precedence: `; compaction: forced` when `COMPACT` exists; else `; compaction: held` when
   the newest supervisor outcome is held and `pending` is set; else `; compaction: due (<reason>)` when a compaction
   would be due now under requirement 1's rule and guards (the last measured context at or above the limit, or a
   `bytes` pending, with no `verify` set and no back-off in force); else `; compaction: awaiting verification` when
   `verify` is set; else nothing. `compaction_status_line(home, now=None)` takes an optional aware datetime for the
   back-off comparison (the wall clock by default; `hydra status` passes nothing, the supervisor passes its clock).
   The number shown is always the last measured context, never a `pending` value: a stale `pending` of 935,607 with
   a newer measurement of 94,586 after a CLI compaction shows `context: 94586 tokens`, the CLI compaction as the
   last, and no suffix. The substring `compaction: pending (` never appears. `hydra usage` is unchanged.
   `hydra status` prints the line as it does today.
5. The per-turn message carries a digest, not the file. `Supervisor.state_block()` is replaced by
   `Supervisor.state_digest(events)` and `turn()` prepends its result for every engine (Claude per-turn, Claude
   persistent, Codex; `invoke_codex` no longer builds its own `# factory/state.json` prefix). The digest is:
   a header line `# factory/state.json (digest of <path>; read the file before acting on a track)`; `updated: <value>`;
   `## tracks` with one line per track, in file order, `- <id>: now: <now> | waits_on: <waits_on> | next_action:
   <next_action>` where each field is the track's string collapsed to single spaces and cut to 240 characters
   (`now` falls back to `stage` then `status` as `hydra status` does; a missing field prints `-`); `## founder
   decisions (<total>; newest <k> below, the rest in the file)` followed by the last 10 entries of
   `founder_decisions_in_force` verbatim as `- ` lines; `## next_gate` and `## waits_on` with those top-level values
   when present; then `## tracks touched by this batch` with the full JSON (indent 1, the file's key order) of every
   selected track under a `### <id>` heading, or `none`; and the terminator line `## end of state digest`, always the
   digest's last line (the acceptance measures the digest from its header line to this line). A track is selected
   when any of: (a) an event's
   `payload.thread_ts` (or the event id when it is a Slack ts) equals a string value anywhere inside the track entry
   (recursive scan of dicts and lists, exact match or as a `.`-stripped ts); (b) the first line of an event's text
   matches `assignee: <name> | track: <id>` with that id; (c) an event's text starts with `continue <id>:`; (d)
   `work-status.json` names the track in `track`. The assignee line is matched on the event text's first line after leading Slack mentions (`<@U...>` tokens)
   and whitespace are stripped, against `^assignee:\s*(\S+)\s*\|\s*track:\s*([A-Za-z0-9_.-]+)\s*$`; both the plain
   and the mention-prefixed forms select the track. Selection order: events in batch order, and within an event the
   rules (a), (b), (c) in that order, then (d) last; a track already selected is not selected again (its first
   position stands); the `### <id>` sections appear in selection order. The digest alone is capped at 36,000 bytes
   (UTF-8); the events block and the engine framing are outside that budget and are never cut to make room (an
   oversized event text is passed through as today), so a turn with event texts under 2,000 bytes hands the engine a
   complete message under 40,000 bytes, which is what the acceptance measures. The
   mandatory part (header, `updated`, track lines, decisions, `next_gate`, `waits_on`) is bounded first: each decision
   entry is cut to 600 characters and `next_gate`/`waits_on` to 600; when the mandatory part alone exceeds 30,000
   bytes, decisions are dropped from the oldest down to three, then every track line is reduced to `now` cut to 120
   characters, then to 60, then to the bare `- <id>` line, in that order, until it fits, and the header gains
   ` (mandatory part shortened)`; every track id always appears. When even the bare id lines with three decisions
   exceed 30,000 bytes (about 900 tracks of 32-byte ids; the supported range is up to 400 tracks with ids of at most
   64 bytes of UTF-8, which always fits; ids are bounded in bytes, as the cap is), the digest is emitted over the cap with the header
   ` (mandatory part over budget)` and one `log` line, never an exception. The full entries use what remains of the
   36,000; when the selection does not fit, the
   last-selected full entries are dropped in reverse selection order until it fits and the header gains
   ` (truncated: <n> touched track(s) left out)`. Every turn record in `logs/turns.jsonl`, of every engine and the
   `compaction` records included, carries `message_bytes`, the UTF-8 length of the message handed to the engine for
   that record. CLAUDE.md's standing rule that the manager reads `state.json` before acting is unchanged; the digest
   header names the path.
